What a doula does

Support that leaves room for your voice.

A birth doula gives continuous emotional, physical, and informational support before, during, and shortly after birth. Doula care is non-medical: I work alongside your midwife, nurses, or physician, never in place of them.

  • i.

    You are heard

    I listen first. Your values, your questions, and your hopes set the direction of our work together.

  • ii.

    You are held

    Continuous, calm support through labor—and a partner who knows when to step back and let your people care for you.

  • iii.

    You are prepared

    Evidence-based information in plain language, and time to think through your options without pressure.

What the research says

Why continuous support matters.

In a 2017 Cochrane review of 26 trials and 15,858 women, continuous support during labor was linked to:

  • 25%

    lower relative risk of cesarean birth

  • 41min

    shorter labor, on average

  • 31%

    lower relative risk of a negative birth experience

The review found no evidence of harm. These are group averages from research, not promises for any one birth, and Cochrane rated the certainty of the evidence as low. Read the Cochrane summary
