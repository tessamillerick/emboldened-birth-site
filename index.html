Meet Tessa

A steady presence for your birth.

I came to this work through my own births. I’ve written birth plans, learned to speak up for myself in the birth room, and felt firsthand what it means to be truly heard—and what it costs when you’re not.

That experience is why I’m here: to support pregnant people in having the empowered, transformative birth they want, not the birth anyone else decides for them.

What that looks like with me: a warm, steady presence who listens first and follows your lead. Your values and your voice set the course. My job is to honor them—sharing evidence-based information honestly, and speaking up alongside you when it matters.

Birth doula package

Support from pregnancy through the first days home.

One continuous package, built around the way you want to give birth. We’ll start with your hopes—not a script.

  • Prenatal visits. One to two visits to talk through your hopes, questions, and birth preferences, and to practice comfort measures together.
  • On-call support. I’m on call for labor beginning two weeks before your due date and continuing two weeks after, with phone and text support during business hours (8am–5pm).
  • Continuous labor support. I join you once labor is established and you’re ready for support, and stay through birth and the first hours after, until your family is settled.
  • A postpartum visit. A follow-up visit at home to debrief your birth, check in on feeding, and connect you with local referrals.
  • A backup doula plan. I’ll name a backup before services begin, so support continues if I’m unable to attend or if labor runs long.
  • Information and advocacy. Evidence-based information, calm advocacy, and support for your voice in the care room—never decisions made for you.

Scheduled cesarean births are welcome. Doula care is non-medical and works alongside your midwife, nurse, or physician.
