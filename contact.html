A helpful first note

Your due date, where you’re planning to give birth, and what kind of support you’re hoping for. A few lines is plenty—we’ll talk through the rest at your consultation.

Email isn’t monitored around the clock. If you’re in labor or have an urgent medical concern, contact your midwife or physician, or call 911.

What happens next

Three simple steps.

01

You send a note

Tell me a little about your pregnancy and what you’re hoping for. A few lines is enough.

02

We talk

We’ll find a time to talk about your birth, your questions, and how I work.

03

You decide

If it feels right, we’ll review the agreement and reserve your due date. If not, I’ll point you toward other local resources.

Prefer to write?

A short email is perfect.

You can email me directly at hello@emboldenedbirth.com. Include your due date and the best way to reach you, and I’ll get back to you as soon as I can.

Please don’t send private medical details by email. We can talk through anything sensitive at your consultation.
