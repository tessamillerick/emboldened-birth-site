A helpful first note

I’d love to hear a little about you, like when your baby is due, where you’re planning to give birth, and what kind of support you’re hoping for. Just a few lines is plenty, and we’ll talk through everything else together at your free consultation.

Start your note

Please don’t send private medical details by email. We can talk through anything sensitive when we speak.

Messages aren’t monitored around the clock. If you’re in labor or have an urgent medical concern, contact your midwife or physician, or call 911.

What happens next

Three simple steps.

  1. You send a note

    Tell me a little about your pregnancy and what you’re hoping for. A few lines is enough.

  2. We talk

    At your free consultation, we’ll talk about your birth, your questions, and how I work.

  3. You decide

    If it feels right, we’ll review the agreement and reserve your due date. If not, I’ll point you toward other local resources.
