Resources

Local support for your growing family.

Places in and around Lake County that I trust to support you through pregnancy and the early weeks at home. Open a category to see who to contact.

Emotional and peer support

Mother-Wise

Free, judgment-free peer support for Lake County mothers and caregivers, with a focus on maternal mental health. They offer in-person groups around the county, an online group, a donations closet, and a car seat program. Getting connected during pregnancy is ideal, but you’re welcome any time.

  • 190 N. Main Street, Lakeport, CA 95453
  • (707) 349-1210
  • support@mother-wise.org
  • mother-wise.org

Baby Village (Sonoma County)

Free support circles for parents and babies, run by the nonprofit Better Beginnings for Babies and led by lactation consultants and specialists. Breast- or bottle-feeding, first baby or fourth, everyone is welcome.

  • Sebastopol: Tuesdays, 11am · Sebastopol Ballet School, 8196 Bodega Avenue
  • Santa Rosa: Thursdays, 12:30pm · Revolution Yoga, 748 Montecito Boulevard
  • Healdsburg: Fridays, 11:15am · Healdsburg Library, 139 Piper Street
  • Petaluma: Fridays, 11:30am · Petaluma Library Community Room, 100 Fairgrounds Drive
  • (707) 902-3031 · betterbeginnings4babies@gmail.com
  • Current schedule
Nutrition and family assistance

WIC (Women, Infants, and Children)

Healthy food, nutrition education, and breastfeeding support for pregnant and postpartum parents, infants, and young children who qualify. Lake County has two offices, and Sonoma County has an office in Santa Rosa.

  • Lakeport: 831 Bevins Street, Lakeport, CA 95453 · (707) 263-5253
  • Clearlake: 14085-4 Lakeshore Drive, Clearlake, CA 95422 · (707) 994-1151
  • Lake County toll-free: (877) 942-2220
  • Santa Rosa (Sonoma County): 1450 Guerneville Road, Santa Rosa, CA 95403 · (707) 565-6590 or toll-free (800) 816-3663 · Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm, by appointment

Better Beginnings for Babies: lactation appointments

Free one-on-one feeding support in Santa Rosa on Mondays, by appointment, from the nonprofit that runs Baby Village. Breast- or bottle-feeding, you’re welcome. Spanish-speaking support is available.

  • Call to schedule: (707) 902-3031
  • betterbeginnings4babies@gmail.com
  • betterbeginningsforbabies.org
Prenatal and postpartum massage

Goddess of Youth

A day spa offering prenatal massage in any trimester, as well as postpartum massage.

  • 21248 Highway 175, Suite 5, Middletown, CA 95461
  • (707) 396-2117
Chiropractic care in pregnancy

Acorn Chiropractic Club

A family chiropractic practice with Webster-certified chiropractors, trained in pregnancy care that supports pelvic balance and comfort. There isn’t a Webster-certified chiropractor in Lake County yet; these are the closest locations.

  • Santa Rosa: 719 Orchard Street, Santa Rosa, CA 95404 · (707) 708-4466
  • Petaluma: 141 2nd Street, Petaluma, CA 94952 · (707) 200-4466
  • Napa: 1700 Soscol Avenue, Suite 1, Napa, CA 94559 · (707) 690-6900
  • acornchiropractic.com
Pelvic floor physical therapy

Balanced Body Pelvic & Orthopedic Physical Therapy

In-home pelvic floor physical therapy with Dr. Natalie Shobeiri, PT, DPT, for prenatal and postpartum recovery, pelvic pain, leaking, and prolapse. She brings the table and equipment to you for one-hour, one-on-one sessions. Start with a free 15-minute phone or video consultation. She visits homes in Santa Rosa, Healdsburg, Sebastopol, Petaluma, Rohnert Park, Cotati, Glen Ellen, and Sonoma; if you live farther out, ask about the consultation.

  • (805) 284-8055
  • balancedbodypopt.com

These are independent organizations, not part of Emboldened Birth. Please contact them directly to confirm hours and services. In an emergency, call 911. If you know a resource that should be here, I’d love to hear about it.
