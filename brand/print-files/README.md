# Print files for hats and shirts

Logo + "Emboldened Birth" artwork for printing or embroidering on hats, shirts and other merch.
Send the print shop the **PDF**. If they ask for an image instead, send the **PNG**
(transparent background, sharp up to about 12 inches wide). The **SVG** is the same artwork
for anyone who edits vector files. All lettering is converted to shapes, so no font is needed.

## Layouts
- **stacked**: logo above the name. Shirt left chest, shirt front, tote bags.
- **horizontal**: logo beside the name. Front of a hat.

## Versions
- **Full color** (this folder): the site's logo colors.
  - `for-light-fabric`: green, brass and copper.
  - `for-dark-fabric`: cream and brass.
- **One color** (`one-color/`): cheaper for screen printing, where shops may charge per color.
  - `for-light-fabric`: all copper `#9a5b33` (close to Pantone 7516 C; confirm against the shop's swatch).
  - `for-dark-fabric`: all cream `#f5f0e3`.
  - The leaves are rearranged so none overlap and each sits beside the stem, since overlapping
    leaves would merge together in a single color.

`preview-on-fabric.png` and `one-color/preview-one-color-on-fabric.png` show every version on white and dark green.

## Notes for the print shop
- Embroidery: the stems and arch are thin. If the hat logo is under about 2.5 inches wide, ask the
  shop to thicken them a little when they digitize it.
