Privacy

Your information, handled with care.

This page explains what this website does—and does not—collect.

What this site collects

This website does not set cookies, use analytics or advertising trackers, or include a contact form. It does not ask you to create an account or enter personal information.

It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical information, such as IP addresses, in server logs as part of hosting. Those logs are controlled by GitHub, not by Emboldened Birth.

If you contact me

If you email, call, or text, I receive the information you choose to share. I use it to respond to you and, if we work together, to provide doula services. I don’t sell your information or add you to a mailing list.

Please don’t send private medical details by email. We can talk through anything sensitive at your consultation.

Links to other services

This site may link to other websites or services, such as email or phone providers, or the research summary on the home page. Their privacy practices are their own, and I encourage you to review them.

Questions

If you have a question about this page, email hello@emboldenedbirth.com.
