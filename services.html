The birth package

What’s included

Every family’s birth is different, but the support I offer is continuous. We start before labor, stay connected through it, and check in after you’re home.

  • Prenatal visits. One to two visits to talk through your hopes, questions, and birth preferences, and to practice comfort measures together.
  • On-call support. I’m on call for labor beginning two weeks before your due date and continuing two weeks after, with phone and text support during business hours (8am–5pm).
  • Continuous labor support. I join you once labor is established and you’re ready for support, and stay through birth and the first hours after, until your family is settled.
  • A postpartum visit. A follow-up visit at home to debrief your birth, check in on feeding, and connect you with local referrals.
  • A backup doula plan. I’ll name a backup before services begin, so support continues if I’m unable to attend or if labor runs long.
  • Information and advocacy. Evidence-based information, calm advocacy, and support for your voice in the care room—never decisions made for you.

Scheduled cesarean births are welcome. Doula care is non-medical and works alongside your midwife, nurse, or physician.

Good to know

Questions families often ask.

What does a birth doula do?

A doula provides non-medical emotional, physical, and informational support before, during, and shortly after birth. I help you prepare, stay comfortable, understand your options, and communicate with your care team. I don’t perform clinical tasks, diagnose, or make medical decisions for you.

Do you replace my partner or care team?

No. I work alongside your partner, midwife, nurses, and physician. Your partner isn’t competing with me—I help them support you in the way that feels right for your family.

Where can you support me?

I support hospital, birth center, and home births with a qualified midwife present. I don’t attend unassisted home births. If you’re planning a scheduled cesarean, I can still support you before, during, and after.

What does it cost?

The introductory rate is $750 for the birth package. A $250 deposit reserves your due date, and the $500 balance is due by 36 weeks. We’ll review the full services agreement—including cancellation and refund terms—before you book.

Do you offer postpartum or lactation care?

Not yet. I’m currently focused on birth support. Postpartum and lactation offerings are planned for later, and I’m happy to share local referrals in the meantime.

Is doula care covered by insurance?

Right now, Emboldened Birth works on a private-pay basis. If that changes, I’ll share the details here.
